## **Slide 2: Problem Statement**

### **The Legal Awareness Gap**

- Mediation is a **faster, cost-effective alternative** to court cases, but **awareness is low**.
- Citizens, businesses, and institutions **lack access to structured mediation resources**.
- **Language barriers** make it difficult for people to understand **legal processes** in India.
- **Goal:** Build a **multilingual AI-powered platform** that educates, assists, and connects users with mediators.

**🔹 Visual:** Infographic showing the traditional legal process vs. mediation benefits.

---

## **Slide 3: Our Solution**

### **MediZen – AI-Powered Mediation Awareness Platform**

✅ **AI Chatbot & Voice Assistant** – Educates users about mediation in **regional languages**.\
✅ **Legal Document Generator** – Auto-fills mediation request forms based on user input.\
✅ **Mediator Directory** – Connects users with certified mediators online.\
✅ **Gamified Learning & Interactive Case Studies** – Engages users in real-world mediation scenarios.

**🔹 Visual:** A simple flowchart showing how the platform guides users from legal inquiry to mediation.

---

## **Slide 4: How It Works**

1️⃣ **User asks a legal question** (via text or speech).\
2️⃣ **AI Chatbot answers** in the user’s **regional language** using **Bhashini API**.\
3️⃣ **If mediation is recommended**, AI helps generate a **mediation request document**.\
4️⃣ **Users get connected to mediators** for dispute resolution.

**🔹 Visual:** Process diagram with icons representing user queries, AI responses, document generation, and mediator connection.
